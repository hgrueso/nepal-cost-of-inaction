# Budget Comparison Figures — Source Documentation

**Purpose:** Document the exact sources, values, and fiscal years for every
government budget figure used in the Cost-of-Inaction (CoI) comparison, so the
numbers can be verified and the methodology defended to the Ministry of Finance.

**Last updated:** June 2026
**Maintained by:** Hernando Grueso (UNICEF)

---

## CORRECTION LOG — read this first

### What the earlier version showed
The slide originally compared the cost of inaction (NPR 8.1 billion) against a
**"health-sector budget of NPR 84 billion,"** stated as FY2024/25, yielding
**CoI ≈ 10% of the health budget.**

### The error
Two issues were identified on review:

1. **Wrong fiscal year.** The NPR 83.99 billion figure is the **FY2023/24**
   health allocation, *not* FY2024/25. The correct FY2024/25 Ministry of Health
   and Population allocation is **NPR 86.24 billion**.

2. **Risk of confusion with a different series.** A separate, widely-cited
   figure of "NPR 84 billion" refers to the **local-government health transfer
   tier only** (not the total health budget). Using "84" without a clear label
   risked conflating the total health budget with the local-government transfer.

### The correction
- Health figure updated: **NPR 84B (mislabelled) → NPR 86.24B (FY2024/25, total
  MoHP allocation).**
- Recomputed ratio: 8.1 / 86.24 = **9.4%** (previously stated as ~10%).
- A second, complementary comparison was added against the **social security
  allowance budget** (per UNICEF Nepal CO suggestion to use a social-protection
  comparator rather than, or alongside, health).
- Education figure also corrected: **NPR 197B → NPR 203.66B** (the MoF Budget
  Speech FY2024/25 states Rs 203 billion 660 million for the Ministry of
  Education, Science and Technology).

The slide retains the old value (struck through / annotated) to maintain an
honest record of the revision.

### Context / cross-checks
- Health budget = **4.63% of the total national budget** in FY2024/25 (confirms
  86.24B against the ~NPR 1,860B total budget).
- FY2023/24 health = 83.99B (the figure previously mislabelled as FY2024/25).
- FY2025/26 health = 95.81B (for forward reference; do not use for this analysis).
- Total national budget FY2024/25 = NPR 1,860.30 billion.
- Social security (ALL, broad definition incl. contributory pensions) FY2024/25
  = NPR 298.57B (~15% of national budget). **Do NOT use this broad figure** as
  the comparator — it is much larger than the allowance-only line and would
  understate the ratio misleadingly. Use the ~106B allowance line.

## RATIOS USED IN THE SLIDE (recomputed)

```
CoI / Health budget        = 8.1 / 86.24  = 9.4%   (FY2024/25)
CoI / Social sec allowance = 8.1 / 106    = 7.6%   (FY2022/23)
CoI / Education budget     = 8.1 / 203.66 = 4.0%   (FY2024/25)
```

All ratios use the same numerator: NPR 8.1 billion = one-cohort lifetime
productivity loss (PV, 5% discount, 35-year horizon), from the CoI analysis.
